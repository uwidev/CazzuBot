The purpose of this document is to formally layout the various types of frog and their effects.

- Frog system
  Instead of merely a frog count, users will now have an inventory. This inventory can hold many things, frogs included. When a frog is captured, it is added to their inventory. Consumption is based on if they have it in their inventory and how much they have.

- Frog Types
  \*\* Frog
  The most normalest frog of them all.

On Capture: Nothing
On Consumption: +1 frogs
